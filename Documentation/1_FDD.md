# Functional Design Document (FDD)
## General description
The game will be called: Void Of Dying Stars.

This will be a 2d game inspired by hollow knight. But it is based on a space twist, with puzzels.
This game also can be played on a controller.

Will be made in unity.

## Functional requirements
The player can move left to right, with the A & D keys.

The player can jump with the space key.
The player can double jump if he is in the air and has it unlocked.

The player can dash left to right with the shift key.

The player can slide down the wall and jump of the wall.

There should be a main menu, where you can press play, options, exit.

The player can interact with npc to talk and gain experiences of different areas.

The player can attack up,down,left,right. Based on the direction he's facing.

The player should have a abilities where he does a mega slash. or Multiples slashes, that he can learn from npc.

There should be a map that's well structured and has lore.

There should be a focus bar, that gets filled by killing enemies.

There should be a health bar, that you can heal by having enough focus.

There should be Resting places, Where you regain hp and set a spawnpoint.

There could be multiples puzzles that the player can solve to unlock different abilities.

Gravity switches on the different biomes.

There should be a fighting AI, For example enemies, bosses.

Enemies should drop money, where you can buy stuff from.

There should be sound for multiple biomes.

There should be simple sound effects for hitting, dashing, walking, talking, etc...
